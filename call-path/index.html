The fix nobody sees

The router case is the one engineers lose weekends to. The call connects, both phones show a timer, and only one of you can hear the other. The phone behind the router wrote its home address, 192.168.1.23, into its message, and that address means nothing on the internet. The usual fix is a media relay at the operator: it rewrites that address to its own public one and passes the audio along. In OpenSIPS, for example, that's what modules like rtpengine and nathelper are for.
